=== SECTION: TRIAL RULE, POWER NODE ===

id: T12_TRY_01
title: With the Heat module, a Power Node's energy or Heat is chosen at its Alert
source: designer 2026-11-08 (F_01); changes H4 only while the trial rule is on
map: M1
given: Trial rule on, Heat module on. (0,1): fd:F_01 > fd. Ready Modules as T10_NRG_01.
when: Reveal (0,1)
then: no question (no energy-or-Heat choice): 1 Yellow, 1 Red and 1 Blue wait on the Power
      Node, as T10_TRY_02. Overload starts; the Power Node gets an Alert token.
when: tap the Power Node
then: two pop-ups on it: "Gain" with the three energy glyphs, and "Gain 3 Heat".
when: choose "Gain 3 Heat"
then: its 3 energy goes back to the Supply and you gain 3 Heat (10 at most, H1).
when: (instead) choose the energy pop-up
then: its energy is placed at once as T10_TRY_03.
note: Games saved before this change (save format 5) still ask at the Reveal.

=== SECTION: SCREEN ===

id: T12_SCR_01
title: Energy placed on a chosen Module uses the energy pop-ups
source: designer 2026-11-08 (Modules point 2)
given: Salvage Siphon (or Harvest Beacon, Power Relay, F_27...) asks which Module gets energy.
then: a pop-up with the energy glyph and "+N <colour>" beside each Module it can choose; the
      Module itself is not the button.

id: T12_SCR_02
title: Outlines for what a Module, Bay or Order affects
source: designer 2026-11-08
given: a Module, Bay or Order selected (its fire outline).
then: the units and sectors it would affect have the violet outline; Modules it would affect
      (energy, ready or exhausted) have a violet outline too. Tapped again to use it, the
      outlines stay while it runs, and go a moment after the action menu is back.

id: T12_SCR_03
title: The Mission bar
source: designer 2026-11-08
given: any mission.
then: at the right end of the Anomaly row, always in the same place, the same height as an
      Anomaly bubble: a cell per Trophy needed (Salvage: 5 Power Nodes, then 3 Cleared
      sectors; Eliminate: the named Omega units). A new Trophy flies from its sector into the
      bar and its cell pops in. The Anomalies scroll sideways when they do not fit; the bar
      does not move. Pressing it shows the full objective, the progress and the whole Trophy
      pile. The old phase / Trophy box under the Modules is gone.

id: T12_SCR_04
title: Cards for the Anomalies and the Heat bar
source: designer 2026-11-08
then: an Anomaly's card shows its text once (with glyphs) and has no close button. On screens
      900 px wide or more, Anomaly bubbles and the Mission bar are twice as tall. Pressing the
      Heat bar shows a short card on how the Heat module works.

id: T12_SCR_05
title: Upgrade points in a bubble
source: designer 2026-11-08
then: Upgrade points show as a glyph (a double chevron, placeholder art) and the number in a
      bubble, under the Bays.
