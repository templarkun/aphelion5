=== SECTION: ENERGY ON MODULES (permanent, designer 2026-11-07) ===

id: T10_NRG_01
title: Every colour is offered at once; one answer places one energy
source: designer 2026-11-07; Core Rules 4.6, 5.3
map: M1
given: (0,1): fd:F_01 > fd. Ready Modules: Demolition Blast and Module 2 (Yellow), Charged Beam
       (Red, Module 26), Modules 51 and 52 (Blue).
when: Reveal (0,1); choose Module 52; then Demolition Blast
then: Red goes on Charged Beam by itself (its only ready Red Module). One question offers
      Demolition Blast, Module 2, Module 51 and Module 52 together. After Module 52 (1 Blue),
      the next question offers only the two Yellow Modules. Demolition Blast gets 1 Yellow.
      Overload starts.

id: T10_NRG_02
title: Energy pop-ups beside the Modules
source: designer 2026-11-07 (points 1-4)
given: an energy question with two or more Modules that can take an energy.
when: look at the screen; tap one pop-up
then: a pop-up eases in to the left of every such Module (inside its right end on a phone),
      with an arrow to it and the energy's glyph ("+1 Blue"), and keeps pulsing while it waits.
      The Modules themselves are outlined in the energy's colour but are not the button. For a
      Power Node, every Module of every colour still waiting shows its pop-up at once. A tap
      places one energy; the pop-ups of what is left stay.

=== SECTION: TRIAL RULE - FLARE ENERGY WAITS (setup option, designer 2026-11-07, I2) ===

id: T10_TRY_01
title: The trial rule is chosen at setup, off by default
source: designer 2026-11-07
given: Game setup, the expansion list.
when: look at the list
then: it offers "Trial rule: Flare energy waits on the Flare until its Alert is resolved",
      not selected. Unselected, Flares place their energy when revealed (Core Rules 4.6).

id: T10_TRY_02
title: On Reveal the energy waits on the Flare
source: designer 2026-11-07 (I2)
map: M1
given: Trial rule on. (0,1): fd:F_01 > fd. Ready Modules as T10_NRG_01.
when: Reveal (0,1)
then: 1 Yellow, 1 Red and 1 Blue are taken from the Supply and wait on the Power Node; no
      question; no Module gets energy. Overload starts; the Power Node gets an Alert token.

id: T10_TRY_03
title: Resolving the Flare's Alert places its energy at once
source: designer 2026-11-07 (I2)
map: M1
given: T10_TRY_02 after the Reveal, in the Overload.
when: resolve the Power Node's Alert; choose "Yellow energy back to the Supply"; choose Module 51
then: after the Flare's own effect (a Power Node has none), its energy must be placed now.
      Red goes on Charged Beam by itself. One question offers the four Yellow and Blue Modules
      and "Yellow energy back to the Supply" and "Blue energy back to the Supply". The Yellow
      goes back to the Supply; Module 51 gets the Blue. Nothing waits on the Flare any more.

id: T10_TRY_04
title: With no ready Module of its colour, the energy keeps waiting
source: designer 2026-11-07 (I2)
map: M1
given: Trial rule on. (0,1): fd:F_01 > fd. Demolition Blast exhausted, no other Module.
when: Reveal (0,1); Demolition Blast is made ready; resolve the Power Node's Alert
then: at Reveal the energy waits on the Flare all the same. When the Alert is resolved, the
      Yellow goes on Demolition Blast by itself; Red and Blue have no ready Module and go back
      to the Supply.

id: T10_TRY_05
title: Energy still on a Flare at Overload End goes back to the Supply
source: designer 2026-11-07 (I2)
map: M1
given: Trial rule on; a Flare with energy waiting on it, no Alert left.
when: Overload End, step 4 (clear Flares)
then: the Flare is removed and its energy goes back to the Supply.
