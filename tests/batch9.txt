=== SECTION: GAME SETUP ===

id: T9_SET_01
title: Campaign, Company, expansion modules
source: designer 2026-11-04 (points 1, 2); Core Rules 7.1 steps 1-3
given: Start on the start screen.
when: look at the questions in turn
then: "Choose a Campaign." with "Campaign 1" (asked even though it is the only one); then
      "Choose your Company."; then Fresh or Veteran if the sheet has marks; then "Choose the
      expansion modules to play with": a list to select or unselect ("Heat module
      (Expansion1)"), Confirm with any, all or none selected.
note: CHANGED 2026-11-07 (designer, open question I2) - the list also holds "Trial rule: Flare
      energy waits on the Flare until its Alert is resolved" (see batch 10, T10_TRY_01). The
      Project copy needs the same note.

id: T9_SET_02
title: Extra tiles are placed by the program
source: designer 2026-11-04 (point 3); Core Rules 7.2 step 5
given: Mission Preparation; the deal does not divide evenly.
when: the mission deck is dealt
then: no question. (0,0) gets the first extra tile and (0,1) the second, if they are dealt to;
      the other extra tiles go to sectors chosen at random. Same seed, same sectors.

id: T9_SET_03
title: The Ally is always asked
source: designer 2026-11-04 (point 4); Core Rules 7.3 step 1
given: the first Allied Drop of a campaign; one other Company exists.
when: look at the question
then: "Allied Drop: choose your Ally for this campaign." lists every other Company, even one.

=== SECTION: ACTIONS (Command phase 8.1, Overload 9.2) ===

id: T9_ACT_01
title: No action menu
source: designer 2026-11-04 (points 5, 14)
given: the Command phase, or an Overload, waiting for your action.
when: look at the question panel
then: no list of actions. It says what to do ("Your action.", "Alerts first: ...",
      "Overload: ...") and which kinds of things are highlighted. Everything you can act on is
      highlighted: units and Dark sectors on the Field, Modules, Bay abilities, Orders, the
      Overhaul button.

id: T9_ACT_02
title: A unit opens a pop-up with its commands
source: designer 2026-11-04 (point 6); Core Rules 4.2, 8.4
given: the Command phase; a Mecha that can Attack.
when: click the Mecha; then click anywhere outside the pop-up
then: a pop-up next to the click, titled with the Mecha, lists "Attack (one hit of N)",
      "Scrap (gain N energy)" and its abilities that can be used now. Clicking outside (or
      Escape) closes it and nothing happens. Clicking an entry does it at once (Attack then
      asks for its target on the Field). A Gyro shows "Drain"; a unit with a Bio-shield also
      shows "Drain the Bio-shield".

id: T9_ACT_03
title: A Dark sector opens a pop-up with Reveal
source: designer 2026-11-04 (point 7); Core Rules 8.5
given: the Command phase, no Alert on the Field.
when: click a Dark sector; click "Reveal"
then: the pop-up is titled "Dark sector (q,r)" and offers "Reveal"; that sector is revealed.

id: T9_ACT_04
title: Modules, Bays and Orders are clicked directly
source: designer 2026-11-04 (points 8, 9, 10); Core Rules 8.6, 8.7, 5.6
given: the Command phase; a ready Module you can pay for, a Bay with Integrity, 6 Command tokens.
when: click the Module; (another time) click a Bay ability; (another time) click an Order
then: the Module runs; the Bay takes 1 damage and its ability resolves; the Order is issued.
      A Module you cannot run, a Bay ability of the wrong timing (or a Bay at 0) and an Order
      you cannot pay for or of the other phase are not highlighted and do nothing; an Order of
      the other phase says why when pointed at.

id: T9_ACT_05
title: Overhaul asks to confirm unless the Flagship is Crippled
source: designer 2026-11-04 (point 11); Core Rules 8.8, 5.2
given: the Command phase; Bays at 4/6, 6/6, 2/6; one empty Overhaul slot.
when: click Overhaul (next to the Bays)
then: "Overhaul now?": "Your Flagship is not Crippled: 12 of 18 Integrity left (Bay 1 4/6,
      Bay 2 6/6, Bay 3 2/6). Overhaul uses one of your 1 empty Overhaul slot (8.8)."
      Overhaul or Cancel. With a Crippled Flagship the Overhaul happens at once.

id: T9_ACT_06
title: Concede sits by the Menu button
source: designer 2026-11-04 (point 12); Core Rules 10.3
given: waiting for your action.
when: click Concede (top bar, next to Menu)
then: "Concede?" with "The game ends at once and counts as a loss (10.3)." Concede or Cancel.
      While another question is open, Concede is greyed out.

id: T9_ACT_07
title: Alerts are resolved on the units
source: designer 2026-11-04 (point 16); Core Rules 8.3, 9.2
given: an Overload with two Alert tokens on the Field.
when: click an alerted unit; click "Resolve the Alert"; click the other alerted unit
then: the first is resolved. The pop-up of the last Alert on the Field also shows, in red:
      "This is the last Alert on the Field. After it, you can end the Overload (9.2)." No extra
      pop-up. Once no Alert remains, "End the Overload" appears in the top bar (highlighted)
      and the other Overload actions stay highlighted.
note: Q8 answered 2026-11-04: as 9.2 says, you choose when to end the Overload.

id: T9_ACT_08
title: In an Overload, everything clickable is highlighted
source: designer 2026-11-04 (point 15)
given: an Overload: an alerted Sentinel, a ready red Module you can pay for, 1 Command token.
when: look at the screen
then: the Sentinel, the red Module, the [Overload] Orders you can pay for and any [Overload]
      Bay ability are highlighted; Command phase Modules and Orders are not.

=== SECTION: OTHER CHOICES ON THE THINGS ===

id: T9_CHO_01
title: Flare energy: click the Modules
source: designer 2026-11-04 (point 13); Core Rules 4.6
given: a Flare showing 1 Blue energy is revealed; three ready Modules.
when: look; click a Module
then: "Place 1 Blue energy on ready Blue Modules." The ready Blue Modules are highlighted;
      every other Module is greyed out. Each click adds 1 (shown as "+1" with a "-" to take it
      back); the last one placed answers at once (Undo takes it back). With no ready Blue Module
      there is no question: the Blue stays in the Supply (Core Rules 5.3, Q9).

id: T9_CHO_02
title: Damage and repairs to Bays, energy colours: click them
source: designer 2026-11-04 (point 17); Core Rules 5.2, 5.3
given: "Your Flagship takes 2 damage. Assign it to Bays." (another time: "Gain 2 energy. Choose
       colours.")
when: click the Bays (or the colours in the Reserve)
then: the Bays (colours) that can take it are highlighted, each click adds 1, the last one
      answers. "Start again" clears the clicks.

id: T9_CHO_03
title: Other questions about Modules or units are answered on them
source: designer 2026-11-04 (point 17)
given: "Overhaul: put one of your Modules on the bottom of the Module deck." (or G_35, F_25,
       S_13 ...); "Which Alert do you resolve next?"; Splitter Missiles' 3 damage.
when: click a highlighted Module / unit
then: that answers. Choices that are not things on the screen ("Run none", "Place none")
      stay as buttons in the question panel.

id: T9_CHO_04
title: Questions about units or sectors have no list
source: designer 2026-11-04 (point 17)
given: "Reveal which Dark sector?" from an effect, or an Attack's target.
when: look at the question panel
then: only the prompt and "Click a highlighted unit (sector) on the Field."; for several, a
      Confirm button with the count. The answers are on the Field.

id: T9_CHO_05
title: M_03 and M_06: no Passive menu before the Attack
source: designer 2026-11-04; Core Rules 4.2; unit file M_03, M_06
given: the Command phase; M_03 next to a Bot and next to a Dark sector.
when: click M_03; click "Attack"
then: "M_03 ... Attacks: click a unit to Attack it, or a sector to Move there first." The Bot
      and every sector M_03 may Move to (adjacent Dark or Cleared; with V1_M03_U1 through
      several Dark sectors) are highlighted. Clicking the Bot Attacks it at once. Clicking a
      sector Moves M_03 there, then asks for the target of the Attack. M_06 alike: a unit with
      no tokens to Swap with first; a unit that is both a target and a Swap partner opens a
      pop-up with both.

id: T9_CHO_06
title: Facing: rotate buttons on the unit
source: designer 2026-11-04; Core Rules 3.7
given: Reveal a Dark sector whose top tile has arrows (also: an Allied Drop placement, S_25,
       V1_M02_U1).
when: look; click the clockwise button twice; then take your next action
then: no list. The unit shows two round buttons: anticlockwise (left) and clockwise (right);
      its arrows turn with each click. The rest of the screen already offers what comes next,
      and the panel says "Your next click keeps this facing." The next action keeps facing 2
      (the log says so) and the buttons are gone. If what comes next would show new hidden
      information, a "Done" button keeps the facing first.
note: CHANGED 2026-11-09 (designer): no card ("turn it with the buttons on the unit, rule 3.7"
      and "Your next click keeps this facing" are gone). Only the "Done" button shows, at the
      bottom of the Field, when it is needed.

id: T9_CHO_07
title: Rows are chosen on the Field, then confirmed
source: designer 2026-11-07; Core Rules 3.8 (rows); every card that chooses a row (S_14, S_18,
        S_20, P_16, P_17, P_18, F_28, G_24 and the other row Gyros)
given: "G_24: choose a row through this unit." (three rows through G_24: one horizontal, two
       diagonals).
when: look; click a sector of one row; click outside; click G_24's own sector; click "Next row
      through this sector"; click "Choose this row"
then: no list in the question panel. Every sector of every row offered is highlighted. A click
      selects that sector's row (its sectors turn green) and opens a pop-up naming the row and
      its units, with "Choose this row". Clicking outside closes it and nothing is chosen. A
      sector that lies in several rows offers "Next row through this sector (1 of 3)", which
      steps to the next row. "Choose this row" answers.
note: CHANGED 2026-11-09 (designer): no question card and no pop-up. A long see-through bubble
      with a running outline lies over every row offered ("Choose this row"). A press on a
      bubble selects that row: the bubble turns green ("Tap again to confirm") and the row's
      sectors get the running violet outline. A press on the same bubble again answers. A tap on
      a sector of a row selects a row through it (the next one on each tap).
