=== SECTION: MODULES WHOSE X WOULD BE 0 (I8) ===

id: T13_X_01
title: Counter-Battery, Mirror Lattice and Backlash Beams are greyed out when their X would be 0
source: designer 2026-11-08 (I8); Core Rules 5.3 (master rev 141)
map: M1
given: Counter-Battery Beam, Mirror Lattice Beam and Backlash Beam ready, enough energy.
       Every sector holds a B_03, nothing else.
then: Counter-Battery Beam is greyed out (no Sentinel on the Field); the other two can be run.
when: every unit is a Sentinel instead
then: Mirror Lattice Beam is greyed out (no Bot on the Field).
when: the Field holds only one unit (a Wreck does not count)
then: Backlash Beam is greyed out (fewer than 2 units).
note: Greyed out = cannot be run; they stay ready, so energy can still be placed on them.

=== SECTION: BAYS AND ORDERS WITH NO UNIT ON THE FIELD (designer 2026-11-08) ===

id: T13_BAY_01
title: Bays and Orders that target units are greyed out when no unit fits them
source: designer 2026-11-08 (play-test, I9); a rule of this program only (Core Rules unchanged)
map: M1
given: Company V1, every sector Dark (no unit on the Field). Enough Command tokens.
when: list legal actions
then: Use a Bay offers only Bay 3 (it chooses a sector). Bays 1 and 2 are greyed out and cannot
      be used. "Deal 1 damage to all units" is greyed out; no Order is offered.
when: Company V2 instead, same Field
then: only Bay 3 is offered; Bays 1, 2 and 4 are greyed out (Bay 5 is [Overload]; during an
      Overload it is greyed out too). The [Overload] Orders that target units are greyed out
      during an Overload; "Take an Anomaly card" never is.
note: Flares and Wrecks do not count as units here (effects cannot choose them, 3.2).
      Greyed out = cannot be used; the pop-up says "No unit to target".
when: units on the Field, but none that fits: V2 Bay 1 with no Level 3 unit, Bay 4 with no
      Level 1 unit, Bay 2 with no two adjacent units, Bay 5 with no damaged unit; V1 Bay 2 with
      no unit next to a Dark (or Cleared) sector; "Destroy one Level 2 Bot" with none; the
      [Overload] Orders with no Mecha; C1_M7 with no Gamma that has an adjacent unit
then: each of those is greyed out too (I9 (a)). See the CHANGED notes of 2026-11-08 in batch 3
      (T3_ACT_03, T3_ACT_04, T3_ORD_02) and batch 4 (T4_ACT_01, T4_END_01, T4_CRP_01).

=== SECTION: WEAPON AND AURA LOOKS (screen only) ===

id: T13_FX_01
title: A Bay's shot starts at the Bay
source: Program Spec "Weapon and aura looks"; build step 3
then: using V1 Bay 1 on a unit: Bay 1 glows, a thin bright laser runs from Bay 1 to the unit
      and holds for a moment, then the hit (flash, "−1"). V1 Bay 2: a pale wavy tractor beam
      grips the unit, then it moves. V1 Bay 3: a ring of light travels to the sector and wraps
      it, then the look card shows the tiles. An Order uses the same looks from its button.
note: CHANGED 2026-11-09 (designer): only V1 Bay 1 keeps the laser. Every other Bay that deals
      damage fires a rail slug (it charges, a slug with a spiral trail, two shock rings); an
      Order that deals damage fires a missile (a curved flight with smoke, a fireball). Moves and
      looks keep the tractor beam and the scan.

id: T13_FX_02
title: A Module's shot starts at the Module, in its colour
source: Program Spec "Weapon and aura looks"; build step 3
then: the Module card glows as it runs. Yellow: a quick bolt with a short trail to each target.
      Red: a wide red beam with a shimmer. Blue: a wave that travels and spreads over the target.
      A Module that moves units or looks at tiles uses the tractor beam or the scan in its colour.
      Several targets: one shot per hit, one after the other.
note: CHANGED 2026-11-09 (designer): an effect that hits "all" units (all units, all Level 3
      units, all adjacent units...) plays as one fast bombing, see T13_FX_04.

id: T13_FX_03
title: Energy waiting on a Flare shows on its tile
source: trial rule "Flare energy waits" (I2); build step 3
given: trial rule on.
then: a revealed Flare shows one small round token per energy waiting on it, in its colour.
      When the energy is placed, each token flies from the Flare to its Module.

id: T13_FX_04
title: An effect on "all" units is one fast bombing
source: designer 2026-11-09; Mission 1 Order "Deal 1 damage to all units"
when: an effect hits two or more units at once (an Order or Bay on all units, a Module, a card
      such as "Deal 1 damage to all Level 3 units" or "to all adjacent units", Meltdown)
then: every target is shot at the same moment: Bays, Orders, Modules and Meltdown drop shells
      from above onto every target; a unit's own effect fires its own shot (T13_FX_05) at all of
      them at once. Then every hit lands at once (flash and "−1" on each), then every unit
      destroyed bursts at once. About 1.5 s in all, whatever the number of units.
note: Checked by scripts/ui-smoke.mjs (find-save.ts area: shells at the same time).

id: T13_FX_05
title: Damage dealt by a unit's own effect starts at that unit, in a look of its type
source: designer 2026-11-09
then: Sentinel: two red plasma balls. Flare: a fiery whip. Sleeper: a thin blue needle, then
      ripples. Gyro: a spinning green blade disc on a curve. Omega: violet lightning, then an
      implosion. Mecha (its abilities and upgrades): a muzzle flash and a fast shell. Damage to
      your Flagship comes the same way to the Flagship panel, which shakes and flashes red (shown
      before you choose the Bays).

id: T13_FX_06
title: Heat Wave and Fuse tokens have their own looks
source: designer 2026-11-09; H5, 6.6
then: Heat Wave: the "−1 Heat" chip, then a shimmering heat ray from the Heat bar to the Bot,
      heat haze and rising embers on it, then the hit. A Tick going off: a spark runs round a
      shrinking spiral on its unit, then it blows. A Mortar: it blows on its unit, then (once the
      target is chosen) a shell is lobbed high onto the adjacent unit. A Feedback: it blows,
      then red lightning runs to the Flagship panel, which shakes.

id: T13_FX_07
title: Fuse tokens and Barriers are pieces on the tile
source: designer 2026-11-09; 6.4, 6.6
then: Fuse tokens are red pieces in a row near the top of the unit's tile, each its own shape
      with a white glyph: Tick a round token with a clock face and clamps, Mortar a six-sided
      token with a mortar round, Feedback a diamond with a lightning bolt; a small number when
      there are several of one kind. A Barrier is a pale blue force field round the unit:
      a shimmering bubble with arcs sliding round it and particles circling inside (still when
      reduced motion is on). A hit stopped by the Barrier: the field flares and breaks into
      shards, "Barrier" floats up. The text badges "BAR", "TICK", "MOR", "FB" are gone.

id: T13_FX_08
title: Each unit type has its own reveal (not Bots); Flares and Omega units the biggest
source: designer 2026-11-09
then: after the tile flips: Sentinel: red chevrons lock on from six sides, then its Barrier
      snaps up. Sleeper: slow blue ripples, motes rising, two eyes open. Gyro: arcs spin up and
      fling green sparks. Mecha: a scan line runs down the tile, sparks fly. Bot: the flip only.
      Flare: the dark tile trembles and gathers light first; then a blinding sunburst over the
      Field, rays, two shock rings, embers, and the whole Field shakes. Omega: it trembles and
      gathers violet light first; then a vortex sucks light in, violet lightning leaps to every
      sector around, the Field darkens and quakes, three shock rings.
note: Checked by scripts/ui-smoke.mjs (find-save.ts reveal-Omega: the flash over the Field).

id: T13_R9_04
title: Damage to the Flagship is placed with bubbles
source: designer 2026-11-09; Core Rules 5.2
given: "Your Flagship takes 2 damage" (e.g. S_12's Alert), Bays with Integrity left.
then: no question card. A big see-through red bubble with a running outline in the middle of the
      Field: "Your Flagship takes 2 damage", where it comes from, "0 of 2 placed". A red bubble
      "−1 Bay n" points at each Bay that can take damage (beside it on wide screens, above it on
      phones). Each press puts 1 damage on that Bay (its count shows on the bubble); a full Bay's
      bubble greys out; the last press answers. "Start again" takes them back. Pressing the Bay
      itself does nothing. Repairs (Bays chosen to repair) are unchanged.

id: T13_R9_05
title: Sleepers, drained Gyros and destroyed Gyros have their own effects

      rune circle spins up round it and a column of light rises. A Gyro drained: it spins up,
      green energy spirals into it and shoots away, and (discarded) it shrinks and fades; its
      effect's shots come from where it was. A Gyro destroyed: its rings break and fly apart

id: T13_R9_06
title: The Field is black; an Overload swirls plasma round it
source: designer 2026-11-09

      motion). "End the Overload" is a proportioned flame button (wider screens up to 250 px)
      with embers rising from it; pressed, it flashes, bursts into sparks and a shock ring, then
      the Overload ends.

=== SECTION: PLAY-TEST ROUND 10 (designer 2026-11-09, screen only) ===

id: T13_R10_01
title: Units are chosen on bubbles on the units
source: designer 2026-11-09; see batch 9 T9_CHO_04 CHANGED note
given: "P_15: deal 1 damage to which Gyro?", "O_01: Boost which adjacent non-Mecha unit?", or any
       effect, Module, Bay, Order, Fuse token or Overload step that chooses units (also "Which
       Alert do you resolve next?" and Splitter Missiles' 3 damage).
then: no banner and no card. On each unit that can be chosen, a see-through bubble on the lower
      half of its tile: what happens to it in bold ("1 damage" red, "Boost" / "Repair" blue,
      "Swap", "Resolve" ...), its source small below (not on phones). The bubbles spring in one
      after another, have a running outline and breathe slowly like other ready things. Press
      one: the others shrink away, the pressed one flashes, then the answer is sent. Several
      units: presses select (green, ✓) and a "Confirm (n of m)" bubble at the foot of the Field
      sends them; "Skip" or "Choose none" when the choice may be left out. Splitter Missiles:
      each press puts 1 damage on that unit (count in a red dot), "n of 3 placed", "Start again";
      the last one answers. A unit with two answers on it opens its usual pop-up.
note: Checked by scripts/ui-smoke.mjs (find-save.ts pickmany); find-save.ts pickunit for one unit.

id: T13_R10_02
title: Strength is shown as pips
source: designer 2026-11-09; Core Rules 3.3
then: no "Strength N" text. One amber power cell (sci-fi, round 11) per point of Strength
      (Level + Shields − Damage), up to 4, in a row under the unit's name; Strength 5 or more:
      one reactor core (a ring of lit segments) with the number in it. Flares and Wrecks have
      none.

id: T13_R10_03
title: Shield and Damage tokens are pieces on the unit
source: designer 2026-11-09; Core Rules 6.1, 6.2, 6.3
then: no "S2" / "D1" / "BS1" text. Under the Strength pips, on dark round bases: Shield tokens as
      cyan hexagonal deflectors with a crest of light (sci-fi, round 11), Bio-shields as the
      same in green with a double helix, Damage tokens as red spiked bursts with a crack. Up to
      4 tokens in all, one each; more than 4: one big token per kind (deflector with corner
      brackets, ringed burst) with the number in it.
      Shields and Damage never show together (3.3).

=== SECTION: DRAGGING MODULES, ORDERS AND FLARE ENERGY (screen only) ===

id: T13_DRAG_01
title: A Module or an Order is dragged onto its one target
source: Program Spec "Interaction and animation"; build step 3 (designer 2026-11-08)
given: Command phase; a Module you can run whose run asks next for one unit or one sector
       (e.g. Capacitor Lance: "Deal 3 damage to one unit").
when: drag the Module card onto the Field
then: its targets light up (red when it deals damage); dropped on one, it runs there at once (no
      tap, no confirm). Its X, Overclock and extra payment are those set on its pop-ups when it
      was selected first, else none. Dropped elsewhere: nothing happens, a note says where to
      drop it. A Module that asks something else first (a unit type, a row, several units) is
      not dragged; tap it as before. Orders work the same way (as Bays already did).
note: Checked by scripts/ui-smoke.mjs (find-save.ts moddrag, orderdrag).

id: T13_DRAG_02
title: Energy on a Flare is dragged onto a Module
source: Program Spec "Interaction and animation"; build step 3 (designer 2026-11-08)
given: a Flare's energy waits to be placed (its Reveal, or its Alert with the trial rule), with
       two or more ready Modules of a colour.
then: its energy sits on the Flare's tile as round tokens. Drag one onto a lit Module of its
      colour: it is placed there (the Module shows a ring while the token is over it). Dropped
      anywhere else, it springs back and a note says to drop it on a lit Module. The pop-ups
      beside the Modules (and "back to the Supply") still work by tapping.
note: Checked by scripts/ui-smoke.mjs (find-save.ts flarechip).
