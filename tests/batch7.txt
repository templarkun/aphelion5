=== FORMAT SPEC ===
Same as "Test scenarios batch 4.txt". Scenarios marked "new game" start from an empty
profile (no Company sheet has marks) unless stated, and play through the choices listed in
"when". "Mission ending" = the objective was met and Mission End (10.1) starts.

=== SECTION: GAME SETUP (7.1) ===

id: T7_SET_01
title: A new game: choose a Company; Fresh is automatic with an unmarked sheet
source: Core Rules 7.1 steps 1-9; spec "Scope" (Heat module chosen at setup)
map: M1
given: new game.
when: choose Campaign 1; choose V1; select no expansion module
then: the Campaign is asked even though there is only one. No Fresh/Veteran question (the
      sheet has no marks): Fresh. Reserve Yellow 6, Red 6,
      Blue 6. Mission deck: 30 Bots + V1's 27 starting Mecha. Energy deck: all 42 Energy
      Gyros, shuffled. 3 Anomaly cards. Starting Modules: the first two of each colour from
      the shuffled Module deck (6 Modules); every other Module is in the Module deck.
      Next: Mission Preparation for Mission 1.
assumption: the Heat module is chosen once per game, at setup (spec "Scope").
note: CHANGED 2026-11-04 at the designer's request - the Campaign is always asked; the Heat
      module is one entry in a list of expansion modules to select (any, all or none), not a
      yes/no question.

id: T7_SET_02
title: Veteran keeps the marks; Fresh erases only this Company's marks
source: Core Rules 7.1 step 3; spec "Saving" (Fresh erases only the chosen Company's marks)
map: M1
given: new game. Profile: V1 sheet V1_M02_U1 2 of 2 slots, V1_M04_U2 3 of 5 slots; V2 sheet
       V2_P1 2 of 2 slots.
when: choose V1, then Veteran; again (another game) choose V1, then Fresh
then: Veteran: V1_M02_U1 is filled (M_02 has "Command: Turn this Mecha to face any
      direction"); V1_M04_U2 keeps its 3 marks. Fresh: V1's marks are erased; V2's sheet keeps
      V2_P1. In both, no Overhaul slot is marked.

=== SECTION: MISSION PREPARATION (7.2) ===

id: T7_PRP_01
title: Mission 1 preparation: Threat columns, dealing, a Module, an Allied Drop
source: Core Rules 7.2 steps 2-9, 7.3; Campaign1.txt C1_M1
map: M1
given: new game, V1, no Heat module, Mission Preparation.
when: Threat columns A and C; keep the first Module offered; Ally V2 (asked even though V2 is
      the only other Company); Allied Mecha M_05
then: Threat Level Assault. Mission deck: 57 + Deployment 25 + 8 = 90 tiles: 9 to each of the
      10 sectors (no extra tile to place). You keep 1 of the top 3 Modules; the other 2 go
      to the bottom of the Module deck in random order. 6 Command tokens; 1 more
      Anomaly card (4). Allied Drop - Level 1: your Ally is V2; the only V2 Mecha of Level 1
      or lower is M_05; it is placed face up on a Dark sector of your choice.
note: CHANGED 2026-11-04 - the draft had you choose the order of the 2 Modules that go to
      the bottom. The designer decided: no choice, they go to the bottom in random order.

id: T7_PRP_02
title: Uneven dealing: extra tiles go to (0,0), (0,1), then random sectors
source: Core Rules 7.2 step 5 and its example; designer 2026-11-04 (computer version)
map: M1
given: new game, V1, no Heat module, Mission Preparation.
when: Threat column A only
then: 57 + 25 + 4 = 86 tiles: 8 to each sector and 6 extra, with no question: (0,0) and
      (0,1) get a 9th tile, and 4 other sectors chosen at random (from the game's seed) get a
      9th tile too.
note: CHANGED 2026-11-04 at the designer's request. Was: "you choose 6 different sectors".
      If (0,0) or (0,1) holds an Omega unit (no dealt tiles), it is skipped.

id: T7_PRP_03
title: A tile with no copy left adds nothing
source: Core Rules 6.7, 7.2 step 3, 10.1 example (copy limits)
map: M1
given: new game, V1, Mission Preparation. All four S_12 tiles are already in use (none in the
       box).
when: Threat columns A and C (both list S_12 twice)
then: the four S_12 entries add nothing; the other Threat tiles are added (8 - 4 = 4). Both
      columns still score at Game End if the mission is completed.

id: T7_PRP_04
title: Mission 2 offers both routes; the other is skipped
source: Core Rules 7.2 step 1
map: M2X
given: Mission 1 completed.
when: Mission Preparation; choose C1_M2Y
then: you are offered C1_M2X and C1_M2Y. C1_M2X is skipped for this campaign: Mission 3 comes
      next.

id: T7_PRP_05
title: Omega units placed by the mission card hold no dealt tiles
source: Core Rules 7.2 steps 2 and 5; Campaign1.txt C1_M3
map: M3
given: Missions 1 and 2 completed.
when: Mission Preparation for C1_M3
then: one O_02 face up on each of (0,-1), (1,2), (-1,3); those sectors get no tiles. The mission
      deck is dealt onto the other 9 sectors.

=== SECTION: ALLIED DROP (7.3) ===

id: T7_ALD_01
title: Your Ally is chosen once; a Mecha you do not list uses the Ally's sheet
source: Core Rules 4.2, 7.3
map: M3
given: Ally V2 (chosen at Mission 1). Profile: V2 sheet V2_M1 and V2_M1A fully filled (earlier games).
when: Mission 3 preparation (Allied Drop - Level 2); choose M_07
then: no Ally question. Offered: V2's Mecha of Level 2 or lower with a copy left: M_05 (1),
      M_03 (2), M_07 (2). V1 does not list M_07, so the Allied M_07 uses V2's sheet: it has
      "Command: Bury this Mecha" (V2_M1A). An Allied M_03 would use V1's sheet instead, since
      V1 lists M_03.

=== SECTION: MISSION END (10.1) ===

id: T7_END_01
title: Mission End sorts every pile
source: Core Rules 10.1 steps 1-9, 5.1 (V1 Trait)
map: M1
given: Mission ending. Flagship 4/6/5. Trophy pile: 2 F_01, 6 O_01. Discard: B_01, M_01, G_05,
       S_12, F_04. (0,0): B_02[D1] > fd. (0,1): S_01[BAR] > fd. 3 Command tokens. Charged Beam
       with 2 Red on it, exhausted.
when: Mission End (no upgrade bought)
then: V1 Trait: Flagship 5/6/6. 2 Upgrade points (Power Nodes), then erased (unspent). Trophy
      pile to the Supply. Discard: B_01 and M_01 to the mission deck; G_05 to the bottom of the
      Energy deck (several Energy Gyros go there in random order); S_12 and F_04 to the Supply. Every token returns; every tile on the Field goes
      face down into the mission deck. Command tokens returned. Charged Beam ready, empty.
      Mission 1 is completed with its Threat columns; next: Mission 2's preparation.
note: CHANGED 2026-11-04 - Energy Gyros go to the bottom of the Energy deck in random order;
      the player does not choose (designer; Core Rules 10.1 step 4 says "in any order").

id: T7_UPG_01
title: Upgrades - partial marks, locked, exclusive, Requisitions
source: Core Rules 10.2; company_vessels_structured.txt
map: M1
given: Mission ending. Trophy pile: 9 F_01.
when: mark V1_M04_U2 5 slots; mark V1_M04_U2A 3 slots; mark V1_T1 1 slot
then: 9 Upgrade points. V1_M04_U2 is filled: V1_M04_U2A and V1_M04_U2B become available.
      Once U2A has a mark, U2B is no longer offered (exclusive). V1_T1 keeps 1 of 2 marks
      (partial progress is kept for a later mission). No point is left.
      Variant: Trophy pile 10 F_01 and V1_T1 marked 2/2 -> gain 1 Yellow at once, and V1_T1's
      marks are erased (Repeatable).
assumption: you mark one upgrade at a time, as many of its slots as you like, until you choose
      Done or have no point left.

id: T7_UPG_02
title: Done with Upgrade points left: a warning you must agree to
source: Core Rules 10.1 step 2; designer 2026-11-04
map: M1
given: Mission ending. Trophy pile: 2 F_01.
when: choose Done with 2 Upgrade points left
then: the program warns that the 2 points will be lost and asks you to agree. No: back to the
      upgrade choices (mark V1_M02_U1 2/2: nothing is lost). Yes: the points are erased.

=== SECTION: GAME END AND SCORING (10.3) ===

id: T7_SCO_01
title: Score: Threat columns of completed missions, +5 for the whole campaign, Heat -2
source: Core Rules 10.3; Expansion1 H8
map: M1
given: completed: Mission 1 (A, C), Mission 2y (A), Mission 3 (A, B, C, D), 4x (B), 5 (A), 6 (A),
       7 (A, B). Heat module on.
when: Game End after Mission 7
then: 2 + 1 + 4 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 2 = 12, +5 (whole campaign), -2 (Heat) = 15.
      Variant: lost during Mission 4: 2 + 1 + 4 = 7, -2 = 5; Mission 4 does not count.

id: T7_SCO_02
title: High score fields: Fresh and Veteran; equal or better writes; never in Developer mode
source: Core Rules 10.3; spec "Developer mode"
map: M1
given: Campaign board high scores: Fresh 10.
when: a Fresh game ends with 10; a Veteran game ends with 3; a Developer-mode game ends with 20
then: Fresh field: 10 with the new date (equal writes). Veteran field: 3. The Developer-mode
      game writes nothing. Your Company sheet keeps its upgrade marks; the Upgrade track,
      Overhaul slots and the Ally's name are erased.

id: T7_HEAT_01
title: Take 10 Heat at Mission Preparation; return it all at Mission End
source: Expansion1 H1-H3
map: M1
given: new game with the Heat module.
when: Mission 1 preparation; later, Mission End
then: Heat 10 after step 7. At Mission End, Heat 0 (all returned); the next mission starts at

id: T7_HEAT_02
title: A revealed Power Node: its energy, or 3 Heat
source: Expansion1 H4
map: M1
given: Command phase, Heat module, Heat 8. (0,1): fd:F_01 > fd. Charged Beam ready. Every other
       sector cleared.
when: Reveal (0,1); choose 3 Heat
then: Heat 10 (8 + 3, at most 10: 1 is lost). No energy is placed. The Overload starts as usual.

id: T7_HEAT_03
title: Heat Wave - pay 1 Heat, 1 damage to a Bot; not with Alerts, not in an Overload
source: Expansion1 H5
map: M1
given: Command phase, Heat module, Heat 2. (0,1): B_01[BAR] > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: Heat Wave on B_01
then: Heat 1; the Barrier cancels the hit. With an Alert on the Field, or during an Overload,
      Heat Wave is not offered.

id: T7_HEAT_04
title: Overclock - 2 extra energy for 1 Heat, then 1 Yellow for 1 more; free runs too
source: Expansion1 H6
map: M1
given: Command phase, Heat module, Heat 5. Demolition Blast ready with 1 Yellow on it. Reserve
       Yellow 6. (0,1): B_03 > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: run Demolition Blast (pay 3); Overclock yes (2 Yellow), and yes (1 more Yellow)
then: Heat 7. Yellow spent: 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 (the 1 on the Module may pay part of it).
assumption: Overclock is decided after paying the cost and before the effect resolves.

id: T7_HEAT_05
title: Meltdown - pay 1 Heat to avoid it
source: Expansion1 H7; batch 1 T_HEAT_01 (Meltdown when you cannot pay)
map: M1
given: Overload ending. Heat module, Heat 1. (0,0): B_01 > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: Overload End: pay 1 Heat
then: Heat 0; no Meltdown. Variant: do not pay -> Meltdown: B_01 destroyed, N = 1, Heat 2
      (1 + 1), Flagship takes 2.
