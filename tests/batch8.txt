Same record layout as the earlier batches. "given" describes the screen; "when" what the
player does; "then" what the screen must show. No map: these are not board states.

=== SECTION: SCREEN ===

id: T8_SCR_01
title: One screen shows the whole game state
source: spec "Screen and controls"
given: a Command phase of Mission 1.
when: look at the screen
then: the Field in the centre; the phase bar on top (mission, phase, objective progress,
      Threat columns, Command tokens, Heat when in play); the Flagship (Bays, Integrity and
      abilities, Overhaul slots, Crippled warning) and the Reserve, Anomaly cards and Orders on
      the left; the Modules (ready or exhausted, cost, energy on each, background) and the
      Trophy pile on the right; the question panel under the Field. No menu needs opening.

id: T8_SCR_02
title: The Field shows each sector's top tile, tokens, stack height and arrows
source: spec "Screen and controls"; Core Rules 3.1, 3.7, 3.8
given: a Field with a unit with arrows, a damaged unit with a Barrier, a Dark sector of 5 tiles,
       a Cleared sector.
when: look at the Field
then: pointy-top hexes laid out as Core Rules 3.8; units coloured by type with their id,
      Strength and token badges (D, S, BS, BAR, ALERT, WRECK, TICK, MOR, FB); arrows drawn on
      their sides as turned by the facing; a Dark sector shows "?" and its tile count; a Cleared
      sector shows the plate.

id: T8_SCR_03
title: Face-down tiles look identical; known tiles are remembered
source: spec "Player choices" (hidden information), "Decisions" (remember tiles looked at)
given: two Dark sectors, one whose top tile you looked at with V1 Bay 3.
when: look at the Field
then: an unseen Dark sector shows only "?"; the one you looked at shows "? <its id>" until its
      stack changes. In Developer mode every face-down top tile shows its id.

id: T8_SCR_04
title: Hover (or tap) shows the full card text and the stack
source: spec "Screen and controls" (hover for full card text and Strength); touch: nothing
      depends on hover alone
given: a unit on the Field.
when: point at its sector
then: the line under the Field shows the sector, the unit's Strength and Level, its tokens,
      what lies beneath and its full card text. The question panel lists every legal answer as
      a button, so a phone needs no hover.

id: T8_SCR_05
title: An Overload looks different
source: spec "Screen and controls"
given: an Overload starts.
when: look at the screen
then: the screen turns red-tinted and the phase reads OVERLOAD; every Alert is listed in the
      question panel in a fixed order, to pick from.

=== SECTION: ANSWERING ===

id: T8_ANS_01
title: Only legal answers are clickable
source: spec "Interaction rules"
given: "Reveal which Dark sector?"
when: click an Occupied sector; then click a Dark sector
then: the Occupied sector is not highlighted and the click does nothing. Legal sectors are
      outlined; clicking one answers at once.

id: T8_ANS_02
title: Choosing several: select, then Confirm
source: spec "Player choices"
given: "Choose the 6 sectors that get one extra tile", or the Threat columns.
when: select items on the Field or in the list
then: selected items are marked; Confirm shows the count and is enabled only between the
      minimum and the maximum.

id: T8_ANS_03
title: Amounts and splits
source: spec "Player choices"
given: "Your Flagship takes 2 damage. Assign it to Bays."
when: use the + and - buttons
then: each Bay stays between 0 and its limit; Confirm is enabled only when the total is assigned.

id: T8_ANS_04
title: Every refusal and log line names its rule
source: spec "Interaction rules", "Log"
given: any question; the log open.
when: look at the question; read the log
then: each question shows "rule <section>"; each log line ends with its rule in brackets. A
      refused answer shows a one-line reason.

################################################################################
=== SECTION: SAVING, UNDO, LOG ===

id: T8_SAV_01
title: The game saves itself after every answer and resumes
source: spec "Saving"
given: a game in progress.
when: close the browser tab and open the game again
then: the game continues at the same question. Menu then Continue does the same.

id: T8_SAV_02
title: Export and import
source: spec "Saving"
given: a game in progress.
when: Export save; on another computer, Import a save file
then: the same game, the same Company sheets and high scores.

id: T8_UND_01
title: Undo stops at new hidden information
source: spec "Randomness, log and undo"
given: you used a Bay, then revealed a sector.
when: look at Undo
then: Undo is disabled after the Reveal. After the Bay use (before any Reveal) it undoes it.
      In Developer mode it is never disabled while an answer exists.

id: T8_LOG_01
title: The log can be exported
source: spec "Log"
when: Export log
then: a text file with every log line and its rule.

################################################################################
=== SECTION: START, END, SETTINGS ===

id: T8_SET_01
title: The start screen
source: spec "Other screens", "Settings", "Developer mode"
when: open the game
then: New campaign (auto-answer on or off; Developer mode; an optional seed), Import a save
      file, the Campaign 1 high scores (Fresh and Veteran), and Continue when a game is in
      progress. Company, Fresh or Veteran and the Heat module are asked by the game itself.

id: T8_END_01
title: Game End shows the score
source: Core Rules 10.3
when: the game ends (won, lost or conceded)
then: the score with each part (Threat columns per mission, +5, -2 for Heat) and "New high
      score" when one was written. Back to the menu: the high scores show the new value.

id: T8_PHN_01
title: Phones
source: spec "Technology" (touch, no hover), artifact layout rules
given: a phone-sized screen (390 px wide), dark or light.
when: play
then: no sideways scrolling; the Field, then the question panel, then the panels, one under
      the other; buttons at least 36 px high.
