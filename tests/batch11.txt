=== SECTION: MODULES PANEL ===

id: T11_MOD_01
title: Modules are listed by colour, then by cost
source: designer 2026-11-07 (point 1)
given: Modules of every colour, some with the same cost, one with an X cost.
when: look at the Modules panel
then: Yellow Modules first, then Red, then Blue. Within a colour, highest printed cost first;
      X-cost Modules last; Modules with the same cost in the order you gained them.

id: T11_MOD_02
title: Each Module's rim is its energy colour
source: designer 2026-11-07 (point 2)
given: a Yellow, a Red and a Blue Module.
when: look at the Modules panel
then: the coloured rim on the left of each Module is yellow, red or blue: its energy colour.

=== SECTION: BUBBLE POP-UPS ===

id: T11_POP_01
title: A unit's actions as bubbles centred on the unit
source: designer 2026-11-07 (point 3)
given: Command phase; a Mecha with three actions (for example Attack, Scrap, Expend).
when: click or tap the Mecha
then: the unit's name shows in a small label, with one bubble per action under it, side by
      side, all the same width, the row centred on the unit (kept on screen; on a phone it may
      wrap to two rows). A long action shows its name in bold and its text smaller, cut to two
      lines; the full text is in the information strip and on hover. The bubbles spring in
      one after another with an elastic overshoot.
when: click outside, press Escape, or choose a bubble
then: the bubbles shrink and fade away quickly (about 0.17 s); a chosen bubble's action is
      then carried out.
