id: T14_R27_01
title: Bug fix: the menu's sparks never draw with a negative size
source: the full browser check of 2026-11-22 (a page error: a circle drawn with a negative radius)

      little before the moment it started

note: checked by tests/unit/particles.test.ts.

id: T14_R27_02
title: Campaign editor: the whole Campaign's sum counts each route pair once
source: designer 2026-11-22 (C8: "Count only the first, the X path")
given: the Campaign editor with a Campaign whose Mission 2 has two routes (2x and 2y) and whose
      Mission 4 has two routes (4x and 4y)
then: the Campaign's sum reads "Whole Campaign (X path)" and adds up the mission rosters of 1, 2x,
      3, 4x and the rest; 2y and 4y are not counted. Each mission's own "Mission roster" sum, 2y and
      4y too, is shown as before.
given: a route mission "y" whose number has no "x" mission (the editor's checks already warn)
then: it is counted.
note: Threat columns are still never in the Campaign's sum (T13_ED_03).

id: T14_R27_03
title: "Trial looks" are now "Features"
source: designer 2026-11-22 ("Let's rebrand trial to feature")
given: a game on the Field; Menu
then: the entry is "Features" (no "Trial looks", no "temporary"); the same rows and choices as
      before (tile corners, stack depth, ready to tap for units and for sectors, unit names, the
      Overload's edge, a sector's next tile), with the same defaults.
given: a browser that chose some looks before this change (for example Rounded corners)
then: those choices are kept.
note: later each feature is hidden and given to players one by one as they unlock achievements;
      not built yet.

id: T14_R27_04
title: The Features page on the Command Deck
source: designer 2026-11-22 (option A: "Features page on the Command Deck")
given: the Command Deck (briefing)
when: tap the sparkles button in the top bar, beside Statistics
then: a page "Features" in the briefing's look, with Back to the Command Deck. One card per Feature
      (tile corners, stack depth, ready to tap: units, ready to tap: sectors, unit names, Overload
      edge, next tile): its name, one line on what it changes, the choice in use named at the top
      right, and a tile per choice with a small moving preview and its name; the one in use is
      lit. Under the tiles, one line on the choice in use.
when: tap a choice
then: it is lit, kept in this browser, and the Field uses it in the next game (and in the Menu's
      Features list).
given: a phone (390 px)
then: the cards stack, no sideways scroll.
given: reduced motion
then: the previews stand still.
note: every Feature is open for now. Later a Feature not yet earned shows a lock and the name of
      the achievement that gives it, and cannot be chosen.
note: the in-game Menu keeps its short Features list, ending with "With previews: Command Deck ›
      Features".
CHANGED (designer 2026-11-22, round 28): nine cards (two new: Energy Gyro reward, Overload End:
      Exposure) and 63 choices in all (T14_R28_01-06, T14_R28_10).

id: T14_R28_02
title: Features: six more ready-to-tap looks for units
source: designer 2026-11-22 ("Ready to tap unit: 6 more variants. Be ambitious, but subtle")
given: Features > Ready to tap: units; a unit you can use now
then: six new choices, each kept light (a few dozen points of light at most):
      Comet: one bright spark circles the edge with a tail of fading motes.
      Scan: a thin line of light sweeps slowly down the tile, shedding a few motes.
      Lock-on: six corner marks breathe in and out, like a target held.
      Ripple: now and then a ring of fine particles leaves the edge and fades outwards.
      Corona: short rays of light flicker round the edge like a small sun.
      Chase: the six corners light one after another round the tile.

title: Features: six more ready-to-tap looks for Dark and Cleared sectors

      Lattice: a faint grid of points on the plate, a wave of light passing over it.
      Light rain: fine motes fall slowly through the plate and fade.
      Vortex: specks swirl slowly in towards the middle.

      Aurora: a soft curtain of light waves gently across the plate.

id: T14_R28_10
title: Overload End: Exposure made plain to see
source: designer 2026-11-22 (Core Rules 9.3 step 2: every unit alone on its sector plate takes 1
        damage; "after I press the Overload End button, some energy comes from Overload button to
        deal damage to those units. Or the opposite ... Do both ... another 3 variants")
given: an Overload with no Alerts left; units alone on their sector plates (not Omega, Flares or
       Wrecks)
when: press End the Overload
then: before their damage shows, the look chosen in Features > Overload End: Exposure (a new card)
      plays between the button and those units:
      Strike (first choice): bolts of energy fly from the button into each unit and burst.
      Drain: energy is pulled out of each unit in curling streams into the button, which flares.
      Chain: one bolt of lightning leaps from the button to the nearest unit, then on to the next.
      Nova: a ring of energy particles rolls out from the button over the Field; each unit flares as
      it passes.
      Plate surge: threads of energy run to each unit's sector plate; the plate's edge flares and
      sparks rise from it into the unit.
      Then each takes its 1 damage as before (bursts for the ones destroyed). About 1-1.8 s; a tap
      finishes it; speed setting; reduced motion: none.
given: no unit alone on its plate
then: nothing new plays.
