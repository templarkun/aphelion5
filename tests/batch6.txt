=== FORMAT SPEC ===
Same as "Test scenarios batch 4.txt". "X ready" = you hold Module X, ready, with no energy
on it. Unless stated, your Reserve holds enough energy of the Module's colour to pay.
Light Modules run in the Command phase, red ones during an Overload.

=== SECTION: PAYING ===

id: T6_PAY_01
title: A cost reduction never counts the Module being run, and is counted when you pay
source: Modules.txt convention 5; Capacitor Lance (3)
map: M1
given: Capacitor Lance ready. Demolition Blast and Ion Sweep exhausted. Reserve Yellow 7.
when: list legal actions; run Capacitor Lance
then: cost 9 - 2 (two other exhausted Modules) = 7: it can be run with 7 Yellow. Capacitor
      Lance itself is not counted, although it is not ready while it resolves (8.6).

id: T6_PAY_02
title: Pathfinder Array - choose X as you run it; the reduction is on the total
source: Pathfinder Array (53); unit file E11
map: M1
given: Pathfinder Array ready. Ion Sweep exhausted. Reserve Blue 4. (0,1): fd:B_01 > fd.
       (1,0): fd:B_02 > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: run Pathfinder Array; choose X = 2; reveal (0,1) first
then: X may be 0 to 2: the total 3 + X - 1 must be at most 4. Pay 4. Reveal 2 units; both are
      Bots: 2 damage each (B_01 and B_02 destroyed).
assumption: X may be 0 (E11: "Pathfinder Array = 3" is its smallest cost).

id: T6_PAY_03
title: A free run of an X-cost Module uses X = 1
source: CLAUDE.md decision; Core Rules 5.3; G_38; Debris Sifter (54)
map: M1
given: Command phase. Debris Sifter ready. (0,0): G_38 Wreck [WR][AL] > fd. (0,1): fd:B_01 >
       fd:B_02 > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: Resolve Alerts (G_38 Destroyed: run a Module free)
then: Debris Sifter runs with X = 1: look at the top tile of (0,1) (B_01), discard it.
      (0,1) is still Dark; B_02 is now its top tile.

id: T6_PAY_04
title: An extra payment is all or nothing, offered only when you can pay it
source: Core Rules 8.6 step 3; Twin Laser Turret (10)
map: M1
given: Twin Laser Turret ready, Reserve Yellow 5. (0,1): B_02 > fd. (0,2): B_02 > fd. Every other
       sector cleared.
when: run Twin Laser Turret
then: after the cost (3) you have 2 Yellow: not enough for "pay 3 more", so you are not asked.
      Each B_02 takes 1 ([D1]). With Reserve Yellow 6 you are asked; yes: both destroyed.

id: T6_PAY_05
title: Accumulator Beam counts the Yellow on it before you ran it, at most 5
source: Accumulator Beam (6); Core Rules 8.6 example
map: M1
given: Accumulator Beam ready with 6 Yellow on it. (0,1): B_03[S2] > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: run Accumulator Beam, paying its cost with 1 of the Yellow on it
then: X = 5 (6, capped at 5): B_03 loses 2 Shields and takes 3 Damage: destroyed. The 5 unspent
      Yellow return to the Supply.

id: T6_YEL_01
title: Capacitor Demolisher with no two adjacent Bots does nothing
source: Capacitor Demolisher (11); Core Rules 11.1 rule 5
map: M1
given: Capacitor Demolisher ready. (0,1): B_01 > fd. (1,-1): B_01 > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: run Capacitor Demolisher
then: the two Bots are not adjacent: nothing is destroyed. The Module is still run and exhausted.
assumption: the effect needs a pair; one Bot alone is not destroyed.

id: T6_YEL_02
title: Splitter Missiles - each share is its own hit
source: Splitter Missiles (14); Core Rules 6.1, 6.4
map: M1
given: Splitter Missiles ready. (0,1): B_03[BAR] > fd. (1,0): B_01 > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: run Splitter Missiles; 2 to B_03, 1 to B_01
then: the Barrier cancels B_03's share of 2 (B_03 unchanged, Barrier gone). B_01 destroyed.

id: T6_YEL_03
title: Ground Zero with no Mecha does nothing
source: Ground Zero (21)
map: M1
given: Ground Zero ready. (0,1): B_01 > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: run Ground Zero
then: no Mecha to destroy, so step [2] has no Level: nothing happens.
assumption: step [2] depends on the Mecha destroyed in step [1].

id: T6_RED_01
title: Backlash Beam counts only real Shields or removed Damage
source: Backlash Beam (31); Core Rules 6.2
map: M1
given: Overload under way. Shield supply 0. Backlash Beam ready. (0,0): B_03 > fd.
       (0,1): B_01[D1] > fd. (1,0): B_02 > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: run Backlash Beam on B_03
then: B_01 loses its Damage (counts 1). No Shield is left for B_02: your Flagship takes 1 instead,
      and B_02 does not count. X = 1: B_03 [D1].

id: T6_RED_02
title: Chain Reaction Beam follows the destroyed units
source: Chain Reaction Beam (44); Modules.txt convention 2
map: M1
given: Overload under way. Chain Reaction Beam ready. (0,0): B_01 > fd. (0,1): B_01 > fd.
       (0,2): B_02 > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: run Chain Reaction Beam on B_01 in (0,0)
then: B_01 in (0,0) destroyed; 1 damage to a unit adjacent to (0,0): B_01 in (0,1), destroyed;
      1 damage to a unit adjacent to (0,1): B_02, [D1], not destroyed: stop.

id: T6_BLU_01
title: Satellite Ion Scan reveals a Flare: energy at once, the Overload after the Scan
source: Satellite Ion Scan (51); Core Rules 9.1 example
map: M1
given: Command phase. Satellite Ion Scan ready. Charged Beam ready. (0,1): fd:F_04 > fd.
       (1,0): fd:B_01 > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: run Satellite Ion Scan; reveal (0,1), then (1,0); stop
then: F_04's 1 Yellow stays in the Supply: no ready Yellow Module (Charged Beam is Red; Satellite
      Ion Scan is not ready while it runs; Q9). The Scan goes on; the last unit is a Bot: B_01 destroyed. Then the Overload starts:
      F_04 [AL].
note: CHANGED 2026-11-04 (designer, Q9) - energy you choose to place goes only on ready Modules of
      its colour; with none, it stays in the Supply (Core Rules 4.6, 5.3).

id: T6_BLU_02
title: Tactical Scan - the tiles that are not Bots stay face down, and you remember them
source: Tactical Scan (52); spec "remember tiles the player has looked at"
map: M1
given: Tactical Scan ready. (0,1): fd:B_01 > fd. (1,0): fd:S_01 > fd. Every other sector cleared.
when: run Tactical Scan; look at (0,1) and (1,0)
then: B_01 is revealed and takes 1: destroyed. The S_01 tile stays face down and is "known".

id: T6_BLU_03
title: Strata Shifter - tiles go onto an adjacent Dark sector only
source: Strata Shifter (69); Core Rules glossary "Dark sector"
map: M1
given: Strata Shifter ready. (0,1): fd:B_01 > fd. (0,2): cleared. (1,1): fd. Every other sector
       cleared.
when: run Strata Shifter; take 1 tile from (0,1)
then: the only adjacent Dark sector is (1,1): B_01's tile goes on top of it, face down. (0,1) is
      still Dark (its other tile). (0,2), though Cleared, is not offered.
assumption: the glossary's "a Cleared sector may be used" is for units; tiles moved by Strata
      Shifter are not units, so they need a Dark sector. Taking 0 tiles is allowed (nothing
      happens).

id: T6_BLU_07
title: Gravity Well may put the tiles on a Cleared sector
source: Gravity Well (71), card text changed 2026-11-04

      face down on top of one Dark sector or Cleared sector, in any order."
