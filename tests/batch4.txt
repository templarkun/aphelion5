=== FORMAT SPEC ===
Same as "Test scenarios batch 1.txt". Summary:
1. Records of "key: value" lines, separated by one blank line, starting with "id:".
2. KEY ORDER: id, title, source, map, given, when, then, rule, assumption, issue, note
3. map: a Campaign 1 map (M1 = C1_M1). Only the shape is used.
4. given: stacks top first, "(q,r): TOP > next > ... > bottom". Tokens as in batch 1.
   Sectors not listed: face-up B_03 with a tile beneath. Unless stated: Company V1,
   Flagship 6/6/6, one empty Overhaul slot, token supplies full, Reserve empty, no
   Modules, 6 Command tokens, no Anomaly cards, the mission's own Order only.
   "Overload under way" = the Overload has started (9.1 already done).
   "Overload ending" = you have just chosen to end the Overload: Overload End (9.3)
   starts at step 1.
5. when / then / rule / assumption / issue / note: as in batch 1.
6. "Overload actions" lists the action menu of Core Rules 9.2. Concede is always offered
   as well (Core Rules 10.3: "You may concede at any time").
7. Card effects used here: S_01, S_12, F_01, F_04, F_13, P_05, O_01, O_03, the Energy
   Gyros, M_01, M_02 with V1_M02_U2, Charged Beam (Module 26), Demolition Blast
   (Module 1), V2 Bay 5. They are built early, in stage 4, so these tests can run.

=== SECTION: STARTING AN OVERLOAD ===

id: T4_STA_01
title: A Flare revealed in the Command phase starts an Overload; who is alerted
source: Core Rules 4.6, 9.1; F_01, O_03
map: M1
given: Command phase. (0,1): fd:F_01 > fd. (0,0): S_01 > fd. (1,0): M_01 > fd.
       (1,1): O_03 > fd. (1,-1): O_01 > fd. (-1,0): F_04 > fd. (-1,1): G_05 > fd.
       Charged Beam ready.
when: Reveal (0,1); place the Power Node's 1 Yellow, 1 Red, 1 Blue on Charged Beam
then: Charged Beam holds 1 Yellow, 1 Red, 1 Blue. Once the Reveal has finished, an Overload
      starts. Alerted: F_01 (just revealed), F_04 (a Flare already on the Field), S_01
      (Sentinel), O_03 (Gamma, "At Overload:" on the Campaign board). Not alerted: M_01,
      O_01 (Alpha has no At Overload trigger), G_05, every B_03.
      Overload actions: resolve one of the four Alerts; Issue an Order (the Campaign's
      [Overload] Orders, 1 Command token each); Concede. Charged Beam cannot be run (no Red
      in the Reserve, 1 Red on it for a cost of 4). End the Overload is not offered (Alerts
      remain).

id: T4_STA_02
title: The Overload starts after the current effect; waiting Alerts carry into it
source: Core Rules 8.3, 9.1; P_05
map: M1
given: Command phase. Flagship 6/6/5. (0,0): P_05[AL] > fd. (0,1): G_05 Wreck [WR][AL] > fd.
       (1,0): S_01 > fd.
when: Resolve Alerts; choose P_05 first
then: P_05 [1] asks for an Overload; [2] a Sentinel is on the Field, so remove 1 damage
      (Flagship 6/6/6). P_05 is discarded ((0,0) Dark). Only then does the Overload start:
      S_01 gets [AL]. The G_05 Wreck is not resolved in the Command phase: its Alert
      carries into the Overload and is resolved there.
rule: "If one of these effects starts an Overload, the Overload begins as soon as that
      effect finishes. Any Alert tokens still waiting carry into the Overload" (8.3).

id: T4_STA_03
title: No new Overload during Overload End
source: Core Rules 9.1 "Overloads never stack", unit file E13
map: M1
given: Overload ending.
when: during an Overload End step, an effect says "Start an Overload"
then: nothing happens (logged). Overload End finishes and the Command phase begins; no
      second Overload follows.
note: no Campaign 1 card can do this at Overload End today (Fuses, Exposure, Bio-shields and
      Anomaly rewards never start an Overload). The test checks the building block, so a
      later card cannot break the rule.

id: T4_ACT_01
title: The Overload action menu
source: Core Rules 5.3, 5.6, 8.7, 9.2; V2 Bay 5; C1_ORDER_1, C1_ORDER_2
map: M1
given: Overload under way. Company V2, Flagship 6/5/4/3/2. (0,0): S_01[AL] > fd.
       (0,1): M_01 > fd. (1,0): fd. (1,1): G_05 > fd. (-1,0): B_02[BS1] > fd.
       Charged Beam ready, Demolition Blast ready, Reserve Yellow 3, Red 4. 6 Command tokens.
       One empty Overhaul slot.
when: list Overload actions
then: Resolve S_01's Alert; Run a Module (Charged Beam only: Demolition Blast is light);
      Use a Bay (Bay 5 only: "[Overload] Remove 1 damage from a unit"); Issue an Order
      (C1_ORDER_1 and C1_ORDER_2 only: Mission 1's Order is not [Overload]); Concede.
      Not offered: Command a Mecha (M_01 has no [Overload] ability), Reveal, Drain a Gyro,
      Drain a Bio-shield, Overhaul, End the Overload (S_01 still has its Alert).

id: T4_ACT_02
title: Command a Mecha during an Overload: [Overload] abilities only
source: Core Rules 4.2, 9.2; V1_M02_U2
map: M1
given: Overload under way. V1_M02_U2 filled. (0,0): M_02 > fd. (0,1): B_01 > fd. (1,0): fd.
when: list Overload actions; Command M_02
then: Command a Mecha is offered. M_02's only option is "[Overload] Command: Teleport to any
      Dark sector". Attack, Scrap and its "Expend:" ability are not offered. Choose (1,0):

id: T4_ACT_03
title: An alerted Mecha may Attack or Scrap during an Overload
source: Core Rules 4.2, 9.2; F_13
map: M1
given: Overload under way. (0,0): F_13[AL] > fd. (0,1): M_01 > fd. (1,0): B_01 > fd.
when: resolve F_13 ("Activate all Mecha"); resolve M_01's Alert; choose Attack, target B_01
then: F_13 gives M_01 an Alert token. Resolving it offers Attack, Scrap and "Command: Move
      to an adjacent Dark sector". Attack: B_01 takes a hit of 1 and is destroyed; M_01 is
      destroyed. (0,1) and (1,0) are Dark. F_13 stays on the Field until Overload End.
rule: "When you resolve an alerted Mecha's Alert token during an Overload, it may Attack or
      Scrap" (9.2, 4.2).

id: T4_ACT_04
title: Effects may Reveal during an Overload; the new Sentinel is not alerted
source: Core Rules 4.5, 9.2; F_04
map: M1
given: Overload under way. (0,0): F_04[AL] > fd. (0,1): fd:S_01 > fd.
when: resolve F_04: reveal (0,1); choose yes, deal 2 damage to S_01
then: S_01 is revealed with a Barrier and no Alert. The 2-damage hit is cancelled by the
      Barrier (S_01 has no Barrier and no Damage afterwards). No Alerts remain.
rule: Reveal is not an action during an Overload, but effects that tell you to Reveal still
      do it (9.2). A Sentinel revealed during an Overload does not act in it (4.5).

id: T4_ALR_01

      (it has no effect: resolving it only removes the Alert).
assumption: F_04 [2] is skipped without a question when the revealed unit is a Flare or

id: T4_OE_02
title: Exposure: 1 damage to every unit alone on its plate
source: Core Rules 4.7, 6.1, 6.4, 9.3 step 2
map: M1
given: Overload ending. (0,0): B_02. (0,1): B_02 > fd. (1,0): O_01. (1,1): F_01.
       (-1,0): G_05 Wreck [WR][AL]. (0,-1): S_01[BAR]. (-1,1): B_01[S1]. (1,-1): M_01.
       (0,2): B_01 > M_01.
when: Overload End (no Fuses, no Anomaly cards)
then: B_02 in (0,0): [D1]. B_02 in (0,1) unchanged (a tile beneath). O_01 unchanged (Omega
      units are immune). F_01 and the G_05 Wreck ignore it. S_01 loses its Barrier and takes
      no damage. B_01 in (-1,1) loses its Shield. M_01 in (1,-1) is destroyed (your own
      Mecha take Exposure too): (1,-1) Cleared. B_01 in (0,2) unchanged: the face-up M_01
      beneath it is a tile beneath. Step 4: F_01 to the Trophy pile, (1,1) Cleared.
      Step 5: Command phase; the G_05 Wreck's Alert is resolved there.
assumption: Exposure is one hit of 1 on each unit (6.1), so a Barrier cancels it and
      Shields absorb it. All Exposure hits happen together; units are chosen when step 2
      starts, and any Bio-shield Destroyed effects resolve after the whole step.

id: T4_OE_03
title: Anomaly: choose one met card, put it at the bottom, choose one reward
source: Core Rules 5.5, 9.3 step 3; Anomaly 01, 14
map: M1
given: Overload ending. Flagship 6/6/2. Anomaly cards 14 and 01 in hand. Anomaly deck: 05.
       (1,-1), (1,1), (-1,0), (-1,2): cleared. Every other sector as default (6 B_03).
when: Overload End step 3; choose Anomaly 01; choose the reward "take 6 energy" (6 Red)
then: both cards are met (6 Bots; 4 Cleared sectors) but only one is used. Anomaly 01 goes
      to the bottom of the Anomaly deck (deck: 05, 01); Anomaly 14 stays in hand.
      Reserve Red 6. Flagship and Upgrade track unchanged.
      Variant: reward "remove 3 damage from your Flagship" -> Flagship 6/6/5.
      Variant: reward "gain 6 Upgrade points" -> Upgrade track 6.
      Variant: choose none -> both cards stay in hand, the deck is unchanged, no reward.
rule: "you may choose one of those cards, or none (then nothing happens and every card
      stays in your hand)" (9.3, master rev 131).
note: CHANGED 2026-11-03 - the draft assumed using a met card was not optional. The
      designer decided it is the player's choice, and Core Rules 9.3 step 3 now says so.
      So even one met card is a question (that card, or none).
