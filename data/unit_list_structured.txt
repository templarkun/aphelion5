=== FORMAT SPEC ===
1. The file has SECTIONS. Each section starts with a line "=== SECTION: <name> ===".
2. Each section has a SECTION_DEFAULTS block. A unit inherits every default unless the unit
   record sets the same key itself (unit value always wins).
3. Each unit is a RECORD: a block of "key: value" lines, one key per line, records separated by
   one blank line. A record always begins with "id:".
4. Keys never repeat inside a record. Key order is fixed (see KEY ORDER).
5. Absent key = not applicable / use section default. "none" is written only where
   absence is meaningful.
6. Multi-sentence effects are numbered inline: "[1] ... [2] ...". Execute in order
   unless the text says otherwise.
7. Effect text uses rules keywords defined in Core Rules (Boost, Bury, Reveal, Drain, Destroy,
   Move, Swap, Teleport, Flip, Dark sector, Cleared sector, Row, Horizontal row, Line of fire,
   Targeted, etc.). Do not reinterpret them. "A row through this unit" = choose one of the
   three rows that pass through this unit's sector.
8. "Flagship" = the player's Flagship. "Level N unit" = unit with Level N (printed shields;
   for Omega units, the value on the Campaign board).
   "Strength" = Level + Shields + Bio-shields - Damage. "Deal damage" with no number = 1.
9. ID prefixes are kept from earlier versions: P_ = Sleeper (formerly Prototype).
10. Flares and Wrecks can never be chosen by an effect, never gain or lose tokens except their
    own Alert, and are skipped by effects that hit or Boost "all" units (Core Rules 4.6, 4.8).
    Flares may be flipped or moved by effects that do not choose them (for example P_25);
    Wrecks may not (Core Rules 4.8).

=== EFFECT TEXT CONVENTIONS (apply to every unit unless its text says otherwise) ===
E1. Choices. You make every choice in every effect, including enemy effects and ties
    (Core Rules 11.1, rule 2).
E2. Mandatory targets. Every target an effect names is mandatory whenever a legal one
    exists, including your own Mecha. Only text that says "you may" or "up to" is
    optional. If part of an effect is impossible, do the rest (Core Rules 11.1, rule 5).
E3. Reveal N. "Reveal N units" / "Reveal N Dark sectors" = Reveal (Core Rules 8.5) N times,
    one at a time, choosing a Dark sector each time. "Reveal up to N" = you choose how many,
    from 0 to N. A Flare revealed this way fires as usual (Core Rules 4.6).
    "Just revealed" = revealed by this effect.
E4. Counts and conditions ("for each", "exactly", "at least", "most", "fewest", "most
    abundant", "highest") are checked once, when that numbered step starts. "This way"
    counts only the results of this effect. Wrecks and Flares count as units of their type;
    Flares have no Level and no Strength, so Level and Strength counts ignore them.
E5. Damage on units vs. the Flagship. "Remove N damage from a unit" = remove N Damage
    tokens from it. "Remove N damage from your Flagship" = repair it (Core Rules 5.2).
    A "damaged unit" is a unit with at least one Damage token.
E6. Hits. "Deal X damage to a unit" is one hit of X. "Deal 1 damage to this unit for each Y"
    is also one hit, of the total (Core Rules 6.1).
E7. "This unit" = the unit whose effect is resolving. In a Destroyed effect it means its
    Wreck and that Wreck's sector. "All units in a row through this unit" includes this
    unit unless the text says "other".
E8. Lines of fire and Targeted sectors start at the adjacent sector. They never include the
    unit's own sector (Core Rules 3.6).
E9. "Put it at the very bottom of its sector" = put the tile face up at the bottom of its
    own stack, keeping its facing. Then check the sector's new top tile (Core Rules 3.4).
E10. "Turn this unit clockwise by one side for each X" = rotate it one side clockwise per X.
    Six turns is a full turn.
E11. Module costs named by a unit ("the lowest cost", "costs 3 or less") use the printed
    cost. An X cost counts as its minimum (Debris Sifter = 1, Pathfinder Array = 3).
    Discounts are ignored.
E12. Energy. "Place energy on a Module" = take it from the Supply and put it on a ready
    Module; if no colour is named, you choose. "Remove energy from a Module" = return it to
    the Supply. "Gain/lose energy" with no Module named = your Reserve (Core Rules 5.4).
E13. "Start an Overload" does nothing during an Overload or Overload End (Core Rules 9.1).
E14. "Choose a row" / "choose a horizontal row" allows any row, including one with no units,
    unless the text adds a condition ("with at least one Mecha").

KEY ORDER:
  id, level, copies, arrows, energy_on_reveal, ability_kind, dev_price,
  then effect keys (activate | ability | drain | destroyed)

KEY DEFINITIONS:
  id                 Unique unit code (e.g. S_01).
  level              Printed unit Level = number of printed shields. "none" for Flares
                     (no Level, no Strength; they cannot be damaged, destroyed or chosen).
                     "per_campaign" for Omega (the Campaign board value counts as its Level).
  copies             Number of physical copies in the game.
  arrows             Comma-separated side numbers that have arrows. Side 1 = the flat left side
                     (sectors are pointy-top hexes); numbers continue CLOCKWISE (1..6):
                     1 left, 2 upper-left, 3 upper-right, 4 right, 5 lower-right, 6 lower-left.
                     Absent = no arrows.
  energy_on_reveal   Energy placed on ready Modules when this Flare is revealed (Core Rules 4.6).
  ability_kind       Mecha only. One of:
                       command   = "Command:" ability, used when you Command the Mecha
                       expend    = "Expend:" ability, then the Mecha is destroyed
                       passive   = "Passive:" always-on modifier to Move/Attack, not an action
  dev_price          Developer-only balance number. Hidden from players. Not a game rule.
  activate           Effect resolved when this unit's Alert token resolves (Sentinel, Flare, Sleeper).
  drain / destroyed  Gyro effects. drain = Command phase action, or an effect that Drains it
                     (then the Gyro is discarded); destroyed = resolved from the Wreck's Alert
                     token after the Gyro is destroyed (Core Rules 4.3, 4.8).

GLOBAL FACTS:
  default_copies: 4 for every unit unless the section or record says otherwise.
  Arrow numbering is the same for every unit type.
  G_00 = a placeholder meaning "take the top tile of the Energy deck". The Energy deck is made of
         all copies of G_01..G_09 (42 tiles). G_00 is NOT a unit type with its own record.
