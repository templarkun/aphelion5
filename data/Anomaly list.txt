=== FORMAT SPEC ===
1. Each Anomaly is a RECORD: a block of "key: value" lines, one key per line, records
   separated by one blank line. A record always begins with "id:". Keys never repeat.
2. When checked: at Overload End, step 3, on the Field at that moment (Core Rules 9.3).
   Requirements are checked at that moment only. A Wreck counts as a unit (Core Rules 4.8).
   Flares are still on the Field at that step (they are removed in step 4), so they count.
3. Reward (any Anomaly): choose one of: remove 3 damage from your Flagship, or take 6 energy
   in any mix of colours, or gain 6 Upgrade points (Core Rules 9.3).

KEY ORDER:
  id, terms, minimum, text

KEY DEFINITIONS:
  id        Anomaly number (two digits).
  terms     Comma-separated list of what is counted. The counts of all listed terms are added
            together and compared with minimum. Allowed terms:
              Bot | Mecha | Sentinel | Gyro | Sleeper | Flare   = units of that type on the Field
              non-Bot unit                                      = units on the Field that are not Bots
              Cleared sector                                    = sectors with no tiles
  minimum   The total must be at least this number.
  text      The requirement as printed on the card.
