# Aphelion 5 — computer game

A solo campaign board game, being built as a TypeScript web app. The designer is the user; the rules are final unless the designer changes them.

## Decisions already made (do not re-ask)

- Platform: TypeScript web app; PixiJS draws the Field, React the panels; plain-TS rules engine with no UI code; IndexedDB saves with file export; wrapped for PC and phones after v1.
- Engine is pure: (state, answer) -> (state, next question). All randomness from one seeded generator in state.
- The player makes every choice; the engine asks one question with the full list of legal answers; auto-answer when exactly one is legal, except the Campaign, the Company, the expansion modules and the Ally (always asked, designer 2026-11-04).
- Computer version, designer 2026-11-04: uneven deal (7.2 step 5) is not asked: extra tiles go to (0,0), then (0,1) (if dealt to), then to random sectors. Expansion modules are a select-any list at setup (only the Heat module so far).
- Interface, designer 2026-11-04: no action menu. The action menus (8.1, 9.2) carry `shortcuts` (one answer = an action and its target); the screen shows them on the things: unit / Dark sector pop-ups (click outside closes), Modules, Bay abilities and Orders clicked directly, Overhaul (confirm unless Crippled) by the Bays, Concede (confirm) by Menu, End the Overload in the top bar. Questions marked `pick` (modules, bays, units, reserve), or whose options carry `on` (a unit or sector), are answered by clicking those things; everything clickable is highlighted. M_03 / M_06 Attack: one question (click a target, or a sector / unit for the Passive first). Row questions (`rows` on the question, from `chooseRow`, designer 2026-11-07): click a sector of a highlighted row, the row turns green and a pop-up offers "Choose this row" (and "Next row through this sector" where several rows meet). Facing questions (`facing` on the question) show rotate buttons on the unit; the screen already shows the next question and the next click keeps the facing ("Done" instead when that would show hidden information).
- Energy placed by choice (designer 2026-11-04, Q9, Core Rules master rev 137, 4.6 and 5.3): only on ready Modules of its own colour; no such Module: it stays in the Supply. When a card leaves the colour to you, it is the Module's colour. A card naming both Module and colour places them as written.
- Paying a Module (8.6), its extra payments and Overclock (H6): no "how much of the energy on it" question; the energy on the Module is used first, then the Reserve (designer 2026-11-04, a program choice; Core Rules unchanged).
- Energy Supply: 15 of each colour (designer 2026-11-04, Core Rules master rev 139; `ENERGY_PER_COLOUR` in `src/engine/rules.ts`).
- Q8 (2026-11-04): ending an Overload stays as 9.2 says (you choose when, once no Alerts remain).
- Geometry: axial (q, r) per Core Rules 3.8. r+1 = right, q+1 = up-right. Horizontal row = same q.
- Undo back to the last new hidden information; unlimited in Developer mode. Remember tiles the player has looked at.
- Placeholders first; designer's art added later in the spec's Art format. English first, language files later.
- Flares may be flipped and moved by effects that do not choose them. Module with X cost run free uses X = 0 (designer 2026-11-08, Core Rules master rev 140; was 1). An effect finishes even if its own unit is destroyed. A hit is at least 1 damage: "deal 0 damage" does nothing at all (Q1). A face-up tile put on top by V1 Bay 3 / V2_P3 / V2_P3A is uncovered, not revealed; V1 Bay 3's "Then Reveal" is skipped as the sector is no longer Dark (Q2).
- Energy on Modules (designer 2026-11-07): placed one energy per answer, every colour offered in one question (`energy` on the question: option id -> colour), so a Power Node's three colours go in any order; a colour with one ready Module goes there by itself, none: stays in the Supply. The screen shows a pop-up beside each such Module (left of it; inside its right end on a phone) with an arrow and a placeholder glyph; the Module itself is not the button. Old saves with energy splits are converted on load (`replayGame`).
- Trial rule "Flare energy waits" (designer 2026-11-07, I2; Core Rules 4.6 unchanged): a setup option in the expansion list (`settings.trialFlareEnergy`, off by default). On Reveal the energy is taken from the Supply onto the Flare (`TileState.energy`); when the Flare's Alert is resolved, after its effect, all of it must be placed (Module of its colour, or back to the Supply); anything left when a Flare leaves the Field goes back to the Supply.
- Modules panel (designer 2026-11-07): Yellow, Red, Blue; then printed cost, highest first, X last; ties in the order gained (`sortedModules`). The rim is the Module's energy colour.
- Pop-ups of units and sectors (designer 2026-11-07): each action a bubble of one standard width (120 px), side by side, the row centred on the unit (from the Field's `data-choosable` centres), kept on screen; long labels "Name: text" show the name bold and the text small; spring in with an elastic overshoot, shrink away in 0.17 s before the answer is sent. Designer 2026-11-08: the pop-up sits a quarter of the tile's height above the unit's centre; a tap on another clickable unit or sector closes the old pop-up and opens the new one in the same tap; things ready to use have no thick border: a hairline amber edge (Field tiles and `.can` panels) that slowly brightens and fades (2.6 s).
- Interaction and animation (Program Spec section, designer 2026-11-07): drag with tap twins, no confirm on drops, M_03/M_06 Passive and Attack in one drag, one-Module energy flies at half speed.
- Engine events and animation (2026-11-08, build steps 1-2 of "How it is built"): building blocks report `moved`, `swapped`, `revealed`, `hit`, `destroyed`, `energy` events into `state.events` (`src/engine/events.ts`, last 400 kept, `seq` numbers; no rule change). The Field plays new events in order (`src/web/anim.ts`, steps in `FieldView.tsx`); a tap anywhere finishes them; Undo plays nothing; targets glow only once animations end; reduced motion = instant. Energy flies to its Module (0.4 s; by itself 0.8 s).
